HOW WE STARTED

Hi, I’m Beenish.

For as long as I can remember, I’ve loved designing: sketching outfits, playing with colours, imagining how a fabric would fall.

I’ve always been an entrepreneur at heart, and Deedazaib grew one step at a time, right from my home.

My first customers were friends and family. They told their friends, and slowly a whole family of clients grew around us.
